### Theorem 3.55

<i>"There are an infinite amount of prime numbers."</i> <!-- Original claim -->

For the sake of contradiction, assume there is a finite amount of prime numbers. Then let $n$ be the product of all prime numbers. 

Since there are no prime numbers greater than $n$, then $n + 1$ must not be prime. Now **Def 3.52** implies there exists an integer $x : x \not= 1, n+1$ and $n+1 = xk$ for some integer $k$.
